# Trail (working name)

Close your tabs and AI chats. Keep your train of thought.

Trail is a planned open-source, local-first tool that saves where you are and what you think, from any AI chat or website, in one click. It files each save into a thread, suggests the next useful step, brings you back to where you left off, and helps you reach a conclusion. It doesn't record your screen. It reads a page's content only when you save it or close and keep it, and organizing runs on your own device.

**Status:** design stage. This repo holds the design documents. There's no code yet.
